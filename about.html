My Story So Far

From Balance Sheets to Bookshelves

An accountant by profession, a storyteller by heart, and a lifelong student of every subject.

I'm Sachin Kumar Jha, from Madhubani district in Bihar, now working as a Senior Accountant in Ahmedabad. For more than six years I've worked with numbers in manufacturing, trading, transport, import and export, and government offices. The habit that kept me going through all of it was reading books.

This website is where those two worlds meet. My work taught me discipline, and books taught me direction. Here I share both through life stories, and through audiobooks narrated in my own voice.

  1. 2019

    B.Com, LNMU Darbhanga

    Commerce gave me my first language for understanding the world.

  2. 2019–22

    Marketing Manager, PVC trading

    I learned to talk to people, understand them and earn their trust.

  3. 2022–23

    Accountant, Jetpur factory (Gujarat)

    I saw how a business really runs, from the dispatch floor to the P&L.

  4. 2023–25

    Navodaya Vidyalaya (Central Government)

    I served schools in Jharkhand and West Bengal, helped students and teachers with new government portals, and was honoured for Best Performance (2024–25).

  5. 2022–24

    MBA in Finance & Marketing (A+)

    I studied while working, because learning never stops.

  6. Today

    Senior Accountant, Ahmedabad

    An aspiring CFA candidate, still reading every day.

Why I Do This

How My Journey Shapes This Website

“Whatever field you are learning, you’ll find it here.”

🌍

Every field, one place

I've lived commerce, finance, marketing, government work and factory life, so I know knowledge comes from everywhere. That's why the audiobooks cover science, commerce, humanities and lessons from life.

📊

Commerce & finance, from real experience

Taxes, GST, balance sheets and money management, explained the way I wish someone had explained them to me.

🗣️

Five languages, one voice

Hindi, English, Bengali, Maithili and some Gujarati. I want great books to reach people in the language they learn best in.

🎓

From helping students to helping listeners

At Navodaya I saw how much a little guidance changes a student's path. Audiobooks are my way of continuing that help on a bigger scale.

🤖

A modern learner

I use AI tools every day at work, and I built this website and its audiobook studio with the same curiosity.

🌱 What I Can Contribute

  • Important books narrated clearly, so busy people can learn while they travel, work or rest.
  • Practical finance and commerce wisdom from real offices, not just textbooks.
  • Honest life stories about struggle, small-town roots and steady growth.
  • Motivation that is real: if a boy from Madhubani can keep learning and growing, so can you.
