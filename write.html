How it works

Four easy steps

  1. Open the desk — tap "Write a new story". The first time, sign in with your GitHub account (Kumar3216549) and allow access to this website's repository.
  2. Fill the form — English title (Hindi title is optional), pick a category: Zindagi ke Sabak, My Journey or Blog.
  3. Add photos & write — choose a cover photo, then write your story in Hindi or English. Use the toolbar for headings, bold text or more photos inside the story.
  4. Press Save — about a minute later the story appears on the Blog and in its Life Stories category. To hide a post without deleting it, tick "Hide this post (draft)".
Audiobooks

Uploading audio, the easy way

  1. Tap "Upload an audiobook" — fill in the title and pick a thumbnail picture (a square photo looks best).
  2. Add chapters — press "Add an entry" under Chapters for each audio file, give it a name and upload the MP3 or M4A.
  3. Big recording? Split it into chapters of 10–20 minutes, or upload it to Google Drive (sharing: Anyone with the link) and paste the link in "…or paste an audio link".
  4. Press Save — in about a minute it appears on the Audiobooks page and on the home page.
Website + YouTube

Share on both, the easy way

  1. YouTube → website: when you upload a video, add it to the playlist with the same category name (Science, Commerce & Finance, Humanities, Zindagi ke Sabak, My Journey, Stories, Selected Wisdom from Other Sources or Mahan Kavita). It shows on the Videos page right away in the player, and in the video grid within a day.
  2. Video inside a story: while writing a story or audiobook, paste the YouTube link in the "YouTube video link" box.
  3. Website → YouTube: open your story on the website, press "🔗 Copy link" at the bottom and paste it into the YouTube video's description.
  4. Share anywhere: every story, audiobook and the Videos page has WhatsApp, Facebook, X and Telegram buttons.
