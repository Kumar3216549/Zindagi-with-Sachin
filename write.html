How it works

Four easy steps

  1. Open the desk — tap "Write a new story". The first time, sign in with your GitHub account (Kumar3216549) and allow access to this website's repository.
  2. Fill the form — English title (Hindi title is optional), pick a category: Zindagi ke Sabak, My Journey or Blog.
  3. Add photos & write — choose a cover photo, then write your story in Hindi or English. Use the toolbar for headings, bold text or more photos inside the story.
  4. Press Save — about a minute later the story appears on the Blog and in its Life Stories category. To hide a post without deleting it, tick "Hide this post (draft)".
